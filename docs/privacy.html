Aurelex — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · applies to every Aurelex build, on Google Play and elsewhere.

In one line: Aurelex has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Looking up a word never touches the network — your dictionaries and your history stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Every dictionary you import or install, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your search history, favorites, groups, settings and full-text indexes.
  • Everything the app writes is local. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing about your dictionaries or your lookups is sent to us or to anyone else.

Removing a dictionary in the app deletes its stored copy and its index. Uninstalling Aurelex deletes everything it stored.

What can use the network

Aurelex makes no network request at startup, and searching a word never sends anything anywhere. Two features can reach the network, and only when you use them:

  1. The dictionary catalog. When you open the built-in catalog, the app fetches its list from rg-software.github.io and downloads the dictionary files you choose from github.com. These are ordinary downloads: the request tells the server only that Aurelex asked for the file, and nothing about you or your other dictionaries.
  2. Resources a dictionary article points at. If an entry in a dictionary links to an image or another file on a website, Aurelex fetches it, exactly as opening that page in a browser would. The request goes to whichever host the dictionary names, so its operator sees a request from your device. If a dictionary references no external resources, or you are offline, nothing is fetched.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no user records.
  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, telemetry or A/B-testing SDKs, and nothing that profiles you or builds an advertising profile.
  • No selling or sharing of data, and no third-party SDKs that could collect any.

Google Play and Android backups

Two platform features are outside our control and worth knowing about:

  • Crash reporting. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Google Play's crash-reporting service. We will see anonymized reports Play shows in Play Console. Builds installed from a GitHub release or an APK have no such functionality.
  • If you use Android's device backup, the app's data can be included in your backup, subject to Android's size limits and to your own backup settings.

Children

Aurelex is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the change will be recorded in the project's public repository. Continuing to use Aurelex after a change means the new policy applies to you.
